/ Playground

Don't just watch — build and debug.

The Playground is OpenAN's interactive developer console: a browser-based sandbox for working directly with the framework's building blocks instead of watching a guided demo. It includes all components of OpenAN introduced as following.

  • A2A-T Console — compose and send Task-T, Negotiation-T, Notification-T and Authorization-T messages from built-in templates, and inspect every raw request and response.
  • Registry Center — register, update and browse agent cards, and see how agents advertise capabilities and discover each other.
  • Orchestration & Execution — trace how intents are decomposed, and workflows are composed and executed across collaborating agents.
  • Ontology & Datasets — explore the telecom operator ontology and replay curated request/response pairs from real scenarios.
  • Mock Agents — spin up mock agents to test your integrations without a live network.
