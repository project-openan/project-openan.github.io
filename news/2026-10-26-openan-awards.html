Introducing the OpenAN Awards

News · October 26, 2026 · by OpenAN

Community Awards Ecosystem Recognition

Overview

The OpenAN project is pleased to introduce the OpenAN Awards, a new program to celebrate the organizations and individuals driving the growth of agent-driven autonomous networks. The awards recognize excellence across two tracks: Enterprise Awards for organizations and Individual Awards for community members.

Whether through code, architecture, standards, deployments, or community building, the OpenAN Awards shine a spotlight on the people and companies making the OpenAN ecosystem thrive.

Enterprise Awards

OpenAN enterprise awards recognize organizations demonstrating strong leadership in OpenAN adoption, deployment, and ecosystem development.

  • OpenAN Industry Leadership Award — Recognizing organizations demonstrating strong leadership in OpenAN adoption, deployment, and ecosystem development.
  • OpenAN Innovation Award — Recognizing outstanding technical innovations, use cases, or solutions built with or contributing to OpenAN.
  • OpenAN Community Contribution Award — Recognizing organizations making significant contributions to OpenAN code, architecture, testing, documentation, or community development.

Individual Awards

OpenAN individual awards celebrate the community members whose expertise and dedication move the project forward.

  • OpenAN Outstanding Contributor Award — Recognizing individuals making outstanding technical or community contributions to OpenAN.
  • OpenAN Technical Leadership Award — Recognizing individuals demonstrating exceptional leadership in architecture, technical direction, standards, or cross-project collaboration.
  • OpenAN Community Champion Award — Recognizing individuals making outstanding contributions to community building, partner engagement, knowledge sharing, and ecosystem growth.

Get Involved

Nominations for the 2026 OpenAN Awards are now open and will close on November 21, 2026. Submit your nominations through the Community Nomination page — a nomination template is provided there, and self-nominations are permitted.

For the full program details — award categories, eligibility, evaluation criteria, and timeline — see OpenAN Annual Awards 2026.
