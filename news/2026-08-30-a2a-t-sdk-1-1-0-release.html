OpenAN A2A-T SDK 1.1.0 Released

News · August 30, 2026 · by OpenAN

News A2A-T SDK IG1453 Release

Highlights

This release introduces High-Value Scenario (HVS) Prompt Templates for Fault Management (RAN, IP, OTN, Core Network), RAN Energy Saving, and Private Line Complaint Handling, conforming to TMF IG1453/IG1453A.

It also adds Feasibility Negotiation and Intent Clarification Negotiation alongside the existing Information Negotiation, and adds Authorization-T support with auto-approve, manual approval, or deny policies to keep agent-driven network changes within controlled, compliant boundaries.
