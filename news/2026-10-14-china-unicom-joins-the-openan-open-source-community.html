China Unicom Joins the OpenAN Open-Source Community

News · October 14, 2026 · by OpenAN

Announcement Ecosystem China Unicom

The OpenAN community is pleased to announce that China Unicom has joined the open-source project, expanding industry collaboration across the community and marking another step in the growth of the OpenAN ecosystem.

About China Unicom

China Unicom is one of the world's largest telecommunications operators, serving hundreds of millions of mobile and broadband subscribers. A driving force in China's 5G build-out and an active champion of autonomous networks, China Unicom has been pushing the industry toward intelligent, self-optimizing network operations at massive scale.

What This Means for OpenAN

Scale changes the conversation. China Unicom's participation brings an operator perspective shaped by one of the largest live networks on earth — real-world requirements, validation scenarios, and deployment ambition that will sharpen the community's work on agent-driven autonomous networking. Its joining also signals growing industry momentum behind open, agent-based approaches to network autonomy.
